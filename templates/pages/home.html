About the Practice

Nurtured Story is a relational and sex counseling practice dedicated to creating a warm, affirming space where individuals and couples can explore intimacy, identity, and connection. Our approach honors the complexity of human relationships through a narrative lens — believing that the stories we hold about ourselves shape how we love.

We specialize in working with LGBTQ+ individuals, non-monogamous relationships, and anyone seeking compassionate, kink-aware, and sex-positive care.

About Me

I'm Shel, a Licensed Associate Professional Counselor (LAPC) holding an MA from Agnes Scott College. My work is grounded in narrative therapy, attachment theory, and a deep respect for the diversity of human experience.

I come to this work with curiosity, warmth, and a commitment to justice. Whether you're navigating relationship transitions, exploring your identity, or seeking to reconnect with yourself and others, I am here to walk alongside you in your story.
